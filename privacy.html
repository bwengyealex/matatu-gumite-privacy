Privacy Policy for Matatu Gumite

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Matatu Gumite is a card game published by DJ Makerenda Games. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and the choices available to players.

1. Information We Collect

Matatu Gumite may collect limited information needed to provide online game features.

This may include:

  • Player name chosen inside the game
  • Anonymous player/account identifier
  • Selected profile avatar
  • Profile picture uploaded by the player
  • Total matches played
  • Total wins
  • Leaderboard statistics such as win rate and ranking

Players are not required to provide an email address, phone number, real name, or social media account to use the game's anonymous online profile features.

2. Profile Pictures

Players may optionally select and upload a profile picture. The selected image may be stored online so that it can appear with the player's profile and on the global leaderboard.

Players should not upload images containing sensitive personal information that they do not want displayed to other players.

3. How We Use Information

The information collected is used to:

  • Create and maintain player profiles
  • Save gameplay statistics
  • Display wins, matches, rankings, and win rates
  • Display player names and avatars on the leaderboard
  • Maintain and improve online game functionality

We do not sell player personal information.

4. Online Services and Data Storage

Matatu Gumite uses Supabase services to provide features such as anonymous authentication, online player profiles, profile picture storage, and the global leaderboard.

Information sent through these features may be processed and stored by Supabase in accordance with its own privacy and security practices.

5. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Matatu Gumite does not intentionally collect:

  • Precise location
  • Contacts
  • SMS messages
  • Phone call information
  • Microphone recordings
  • Camera recordings
  • Health information
  • Financial information

6. Local Game Data

Some game information may be stored locally on the player's device, including settings, game progress, owned game items, coins, selected avatars, card backs, and table themes.

Local information may be lost if the player deletes the app, clears its data, or changes devices.

7. Children's Privacy

Matatu Gumite is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Players should avoid entering sensitive personal information into their player name or profile picture.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected inappropriately, we may take reasonable steps to remove it.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information used by the game. However, no internet transmission or online storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Deletion Requests

Players who want to request deletion of information associated with their online Matatu Gumite profile may contact us using the email address below.

Please provide enough information for us to identify the relevant player profile without sending unnecessary sensitive information.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Matatu Gumite adds or changes features. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
